# Citation Style Templates

Quick reference for formatting citations. When in doubt about a specific edge case
(e.g. multiple authors, no author, online-only source), check the style's official
manual — these are the common cases, not exhaustive rules.

## APA (7th edition)

**Journal article**
```
Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of article. Journal Name, Volume(Issue), pages. https://doi.org/xxxx
```

**In-text**: (Author, Year) or (Author & Author, Year) for two authors; (Author et al., Year) for three or more.

## MLA (9th edition)

**Journal article**
```
Author Last, First. "Title of Article." Journal Name, vol. X, no. X, Year, pp. XX-XX.
```

**In-text**: (Author Page)

## Chicago (17th edition, Author-Date)

**Journal article**
```
Author Last, First. Year. "Title of Article." Journal Name Volume (Issue): pages. https://doi.org/xxxx.
```

**In-text**: (Author Year, page)

## IEEE

**Journal article**
```
[N] A. Author and B. Author, "Title of article," Journal Name, vol. X, no. X, pp. XX-XX, Month Year.
```

**In-text**: numbered, in order of first appearance, e.g. [1], [2].

## GB/T 7714 (常用于中文学术写作)

**期刊文章**
```
[N] 作者. 文章标题[J]. 期刊名称, 年份, 卷(期): 起止页码.
```

**正文引用**: 上标数字，按引用顺序编号，如 [1]、[2]。

## General rules that apply across styles

- Never fabricate a DOI, page range, or volume/issue number — omit it or mark it
  as unverified rather than inventing a plausible-looking value.
- Keep author name formatting (order, initials vs. full names) consistent within
  a single bibliography.
- List sources alphabetically by author surname unless the style specifies
  otherwise (IEEE and GB/T 7714 use citation order instead).
